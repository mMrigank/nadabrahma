# Workflows: Director's Mode (The Mood-to-Masterpiece AI Co-Pilot)
*Step-by-Step Workflow for Film Directors, Video Creators, Storytellers & Non-Composers*

---

## 1. Step 1: The Navarasa (9 Rasas) Emotional Selection
Open the **Scene Mood Wizard**. Select the core emotion of your scene:
* **Shringara (💖 Love / Romance / Longing):** Suggests *Raga Yaman* (D Lydian) or *Raga Desh*.
* **Karuna (😢 Grief / Compassion / Pathos):** Suggests *Raga Bhairavi* (Phrygian) or *Raga Todi*.
* **Veera (⚔️ Heroism / Valor / Pride):** Suggests *Raga Durga* (Dorian Pentatonic) or *Raga Shankara*.
* **Raudra (⚡ Fury / Wrath / Cosmic Destruction):** Suggests *Raga Marwa* or *Raga Bhairav*.
* **Adbhuta (🌌 Wonder / Cosmic Awe / Discovery):** Suggests *Raga Hansadhwani* (Major Pentatonic + Maj 7th).
* **Shanta (🕊️ Peace / Stillness / Meditation):** Suggests *Raga Bhoopali* or *Raga Malkauns*.
* **Bhayanaka (🌑 Dread / Psychological Fear):** Suggests *Raga Todi (Vilambit)* or *Raga Purvi*.
* **Hasya (🎭 Joy / Playfulness / Wit):** Suggests *Raga Khamaj* (Mixolydian).
* **Bibhatsa (🍂 Chaos / Aversion):** Suggests dissonant microtonal scales.

---

## 2. Step 2: Intelligent Production Parameters
The AI Music Supervisor automatically recommends:
* **Ensemble:** Sitar + Bansuri + Tanpura (or Cinematic Cello + Ambient Pads + Sub-Bass).
* **Tempo & Meter:** 78 BPM (Vilambit) in Teental (16-beat / 4/4 time).
* **Dynamic Curve:** Gentle Ambient Intro $\rightarrow$ Mid-Scene Climax $\rightarrow$ Peaceful Resolution.

---

## 3. Step 3: Generating Track 1 (The Instrumental Bed)
* Click **[ Generate Instrumental Bed ]**.
* **YuE 2** or **Stable Audio 3** generates an immaculate, acoustic-only backing track locked to the raga and BPM.
* Preview the scene's emotional atmosphere in seconds.

---

## 4. Step 4: Track 2 — Vocal Designer & Custom Voice Drop Box
Choose how the vocal will perform over Track 1:
* **Mode A: Custom Voice Drop Box (15–30 Seconds Audio):**
  * Drag and drop any voice recording (.wav, .mp3, voice memo).
  * The model extracts timbre, breath character, and vibrato speed.
  * Reinterprets the voice through the chosen raga (rather than mechanical repetition).
* **Mode B: Wordless Cinematic Aalap:**
  * Sings on pure expressive vowels (`Aaa`, `Ooo`, `Hey`) with no language barrier.
* **Mode C: Sacred Sanskrit Shloka:**
  * Auto-picks an authentic mantra matching the Rasa, synthesized via Vāgdhenu with zero schwa deletion.
* **Texture Sliders:**
  * Adjust Breathiness, Vocal Rasp/Grit, Gender/Formant Shift, and Gamaka Ornamentation.
* **Click [ Generate Vocal ]:** Automatically locked to the rhythm and harmony of Track 1!

---

## 5. Step 5: Populating Tracks 3, 4, & 5
* **Track 3 (Percussion):** Add subtle Tabla or cinematic Taiko hits that enter at the climax.
* **Track 4 (Atmosphere / Drones):** Temple bells, rain, or Tanpura resonance.
* **Track 5 (Risers & Drops):** Bar-quantized transition FX (braams, whooshes, sub-drops) tuned to $Sa$.
