# Workflows: Track 5 — Risers & Drops Across 10 Genres
*The Dynamic Transition & Climax Engine Powered by Stable Audio 3*

---

## 1. Why Track 5 is Crucial
Transitions (risers, whooshes, downlifters, sub-bass drops, braams, impacts) are what make music breathe, build tension, and explode into emotional releases. In **Nādabrahma**, Track 5 is a dedicated transition studio that synchronizes tension directly with the musical grid.

## 3. Intelligent Features Built into Track 5
1. **Harmonically Tuned Sub-Drops (Tuned to $Sa$):**
   * Sub-drops and cinematic braams are automatically pitched to the project's root note (e.g. $D_1$ at $36.7\text{Hz}$ if $Sa = 142.5\text{Hz}$) so they resonate in key.
2. **Bar-Quantized Snapping:**
   * Risers snap to $1/2\text{ Bar}$, $1\text{ Bar}$, $2\text{ Bars}$, $4\text{ Bars}$, or $8\text{ Bars}$.
   * The peak of the riser automatically detonates on beat 1 of the drop or the Indian rhythmic **Sam**.
3. **Pre-Drop Silence Gap:**
   * Mutes all other tracks for 1 beat right before the drop, amplifying the explosive impact.
