# Universal Audio Roles: Beyond Standard Music Production
*How Nādabrahma Functions as an All-in-One Studio for Film Scores, Voice-Overs, Audiobooks & Foley*

---

## 1. Background Music (BGM) & Instrumental Scoring (No Lyrics)
Not every project needs lyrics. For film cues, video games, YouTube creators, podcasts, and video advertisements, **Nādabrahma** generates strictly instrumental arrangements:
* **Cinematic Underscore:** Unobtrusive harmonic beds that sit cleanly underneath spoken dialogue without clashing.
* **Global Fusion Cues:** Celtic bagpipes + Indian Bansuri, Cyberpunk synthwave + Sitar, Afrobeat polyrhythms + Brass.
* **Ambient & Meditation:** Drone beds tuned to 432 Hz and 528 Hz Solfeggio frequencies for yoga, breathwork, and deep sleep.

---

## 2. Pure Solo Vocals & Wordless Aalap (A Cappella)
Expressive vocals without words or backing instruments:
* **Wordless Classical Aalap:** Intimate vocal improvisation gliding across a raga using pure vowels (`Aaa`, `Ooo`, `Hey`).
* **Operatic Vocalise & Celtic Lilts:** Soaring, melodic emotional climaxes (Hans Zimmer / *Gladiator* / *Dune* style).
* **Vedic & Gregorian Chants:** Pure human acoustic resonance with zero instrument distraction.

---

## 3. The Professional Voice-Over (VO) & Narration Studio
Dedicated voice-over generation powered by neural TTS with automatic BGM sidechain ducking:

1. **Cinematic Movie Trailer Voice:**
   * *Acoustic Profile:* Deep, rumbling, gravelly baritone with aggressive proximity effect and subtle sub-bass enhancement.
   * *Style:* Dramatic, commanding ("In a world divided by darkness...").
2. **Documentary Narration (BBC / National Geographic Style):**
   * *Acoustic Profile:* Warm, articulate, authoritative, highly intelligible mid-range (David Attenborough / Morgan Freeman archetype).
   * *Style:* Patient, informative, wondrous, perfectly timed to visual cuts.
3. **Commercial & Brand Voice-Over:**
   * *Acoustic Profile:* Bright, punchy, conversational, friendly, modern corporate delivery.
   * *Style:* Energetic, crisp, persuasive for ads, promos, and product launches.
4. **Audio-Stories & Audiobooks:**
   * Multi-character dialogue synthesis with distinct vocal identities, narrator pacing, emotional inflection, and natural breathing pauses.

---

## 4. Foley & Commercial Sound Design
Using **Stable Audio 3**, the studio synthesizes bespoke sound effects on demand:
* **Product & Commercial Foley:** Beverage can opening fizz, glass clinks, tech notification chimes, luxury car door thuds.
* **Cinematic & Natural Atmospheres:** Heavy thunderstorm with rolling sub-bass, forest wind through pine trees, crackling bonfire, ocean waves.
* **Action & Sci-Fi Elements:** Laser charges, spaceship airlock hiss, robotic servo whirs, sword unsheathing metallic scrapes.
* **Temple & Sacred FX:** Temple bell reverberation, giant bronze gong wash, ghungroo foot-shimmers, sacred conch (*Shankha*) blasts.
