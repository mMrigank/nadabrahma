# Audio Restoration & Hi-Fi Engine: Eliminating the "AI Sound"
*5-Stage Neural Pipeline for Transforming 24kHz AI Audio into Pristine 96kHz / 32-Bit Masters*

---

## 1. The 5-Stage Hi-Fi Restoration Engine

1. **Neural Audio Super-Resolution (AudioSR 96k):**
   * Synthesizes missing ultra-high frequencies ($16\text{ kHz} - 48\text{ kHz}$).
   * Restores vocal "Air", cymbal sizzle, and Sitar *Jawari* overtones.
2. **Spectral De-Fizzing & De-Artifacting (UVR):**
   * Removes phasey diffusion background noise and MP3-like codec burble.
3. **Transient Punch Recovery:**
   * Detects drum attacks (Tabla, Kick) and acoustic plucks.
   * Injects a clean $5-15\text{ms}$ transient attack to eliminate mushy dynamics.
4. **Dynamic Resonance Taming:**
   * Suppresses harsh ear-fatiguing resonant peaks in the $2.5\text{ kHz} - 4.5\text{ kHz}$ region.
5. **Analog Saturation & Mastering Chain:**
   * Tube/Tape saturation adds warm even-order harmonics, gluing digital stems into acoustic reality.

---

## 2. The Hybrid Anchor Technique
The human ear evaluates the fidelity of a track based on its **crispest transients** and **deepest bass**.
* By keeping the **Tanpura Drone** and **Percussion Stems** grounded in uncompressed 24-bit / 96kHz acoustic soundfonts, any minor AI compression on the melodic layer is masked psychoacoustically.
