# theDAW Feature Analysis & Integration
*Synthesizing the Composition, Notation, Lyric Poetics & Mastering Suite from GANTASMO's theDAW*

---

## 1. Key Capabilities Adopted into Nādabrahma

### A. Multitrack Timeline (EDIT)
* Linear multitrack arrangement with slip editing, corner fade handles, per-track volume, pan, mute, solo, and insert effects.
* Real-time automation recording for volume and parameter curves.

### B. Mastering & 25-Effect Chain (MIX)
* Mastering Maximizer, LUFS normalization metering (-14 LUFS streaming target), dynamic EQ, and multiband compression.
* **Quick Master Knobs:** One-touch control over *Punch*, *Air*, *Drive*, and *Ceiling*.
* **The Owl 3D HRTF Spatializer:** Full 360° binaural spherical positioning of sound elements around the listener.

### C. Forced Lyric Alignment & Karaoke (SING)
* Meta MMS forced aligner times every word against the vocal stem millisecond-by-millisecond.
* Whisper review mode flags word discrepancies with amber underlines.

### D. Lyric Poetics & Meter Reader (STUDY / LYRIC)
* In-gutter real-time syllable counter.
* Rhyme scheme analyzer ($AABB$, slant rhymes, multi-syllabic rhymes).
* Poetic device detection: alliteration, assonance, anaphora, refrains, and meter (*Chhandas*).

### E. Sheet Music & Tablature (SCORE)
* Polyphonic audio-to-MIDI transcription.
* Engraves projects into MusicXML sheet music, guitar/sitar tabs, lead sheets, and chord tracks.
* Interactive play-along views: **Strip** (continuous scrolling staff), **Highway** (vertical note highway), **Page**, and **Chords**.
