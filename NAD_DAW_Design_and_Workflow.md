# Nādabrahma (NAD) DAW: Design & Workflow Overview

## Brief Overview
**Nādabrahma (NAD) DAW** is an all-in-one AI-powered audio workstation meticulously architected for **Apple Silicon Mac M1 Max (32GB Unified Memory)**. It is uniquely tailored for Indian Classical Music and background scoring, integrating profound musicology rules (Ragas, Talas, 22 Shrutis Just Intonation, continuous Moveable Sa) with state-of-the-art AI generative models. 

NAD DAW serves to bridge the gap between ancient musical heritage and modern AI capabilities, ensuring uncompromising audio fidelity with its native **96 kHz / 32-bit float** internal engine.

## Core Features
1. **Multi-Model Generative AI Suite:** Deep integration with YuE 2, MiniMax 3, ACE, and Stable Audio 3.
2. **Musicology Engine:** Enforces Indian Classical rules, authentic Raga scales, and precise Shruti intervals.
3. **Vāgdhenu Vocal Engine:** Synthesizes native Sanskrit Vedic recitations and Hindi vocals with perfect phonetic annunciation and zero schwa-deletion.
4. **StemForge / Separation:** Advanced GPU-accelerated stem separation using Demucs and torchcrepe.
5. **Chimera Orchestration:** Advanced mixing algorithm for aligning multi-source generated stems.
6. **Spectrum Enhancers:** Inject "Hypersonic Air" (20k–48k Hz) on vocals/flutes and "Somatic Infrasound" (10–20 Hz) on sub-drops.
7. **Zero Memory Leaks:** Highly optimized for Apple MLX and C++ inference frameworks to leave overhead room for multitasking.

## Step-by-Step Workflow

This simple, hassle-free process allows users to seamlessly navigate from ideation to the final mastered track.

### 1. Generating the Music (Foundation)
- **Action:** Open the **GENERATE** tab.
- **Process:** Select your desired foundational model (e.g., Stable Audio 3 or YuE 2). Define your parameters such as tempo (Tala), scale (Moveable Sa), and emotional context (Raga).
- **Result:** The AI generates foundational background scores or instrumental tracks. The generated audio is directly synced to the `studioStore` and saved to the `Renders` folder.

### 2. Creating Stems (Isolation)
- **Action:** Move the generated track to the **EDIT** tab and engage **Stem Generation**.
- **Process:** The integrated Demucs sidecar environment separates the track into distinct instrumental stems (e.g., Bass, Drums, Melody, Other).
- **Result:** You obtain isolated layers, allowing for precise structural editing and muting of unwanted instruments.

### 3. Generating Vocals (Vāgdhenu)
- **Action:** Open the **VOCALS / VĀGDHENU** module.
- **Process:** Feed in the lyrics (Sanskrit, Hindi, etc.). The engine decodes the text for perfect phonetic annunciation, preventing common AI hallucination errors like schwa-deletion. You can specify texture, pitch modulation, and vibrato (gamakas).
- **Result:** A pristine, high-fidelity vocal stem ready for the mix.

### 4. CHIMERA (Mix It All Together)
- **Action:** Send all generated stems and vocals to the **CHIMERA / TIMELINE** tab.
- **Process:** Chimera acts as the orchestration brain. It aligns the tempo, matches the Moveable Sa (pitch), and blends the various stems generated by different AI models.
- **Result:** A cohesive, multi-layered arrangement that sounds like a unified ensemble.

### 5. Applying Effects (Sweetening)
- **Action:** Open the **EFFECTS / CHAIN** panel.
- **Process:** Apply targeted spectrum enhancements:
  - Add **Hypersonic Air (20k–48k Hz)** to the Vāgdhenu vocals, flutes, and strings for upper-harmonic brilliance.
  - Add **Somatic Infrasound (10–20 Hz)** to percussive stems (Tabla/Mridangam/Drums) for bodily resonance.
- **Result:** A hyper-detailed, immersive sonic texture.

### 6. Final Mixing and Mastering
- **Action:** Navigate to the **MIX** console.
- **Process:** Balance the volume levels, adjust panning, and run the final mix through the 96kHz super-resolution upscaler pipeline to guarantee flawless fidelity across the 10Hz to 48kHz spectrum.
- **Result:** Export the final, mastered track.
